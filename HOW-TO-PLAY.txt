UBW CO-OP: HOW TO PLAY
======================

PART 1: SET UP (only once, on each PC)
--------------------------------------
Step 1. Open a terminal.
Step 2. Type this and press Enter (it asks for your password):
          sudo pacman -S umu-launcher innoextract
Step 3. Unzip UBW-Coop-<version>.zip. You get a folder called UBW-Coop.
Step 4. Put your three GOG game files into the UBW-Coop folder:
          setup_starship_troopers_ultimate_bug_war_1.2.0_(64bit)_(90228).exe
          setup_starship_troopers_ultimate_bug_war_1.2.0_(64bit)_(90228)-1.bin
          setup_starship_troopers_ultimate_bug_war_1.2.0_(64bit)_(90228)-2.bin
Step 5. Double-click PLAY-COOP.sh in the UBW-Coop folder.
        (If it asks, choose "Execute" or "Run".)
        The first time, it unpacks the game. Wait about a minute.
        The game then starts.

Next time, only do Step 5.

PART 2: START THE GAME
----------------------
Step 1. Double-click PLAY-COOP.sh.
Step 2. A box says "Authentication failed". Click it away.
Step 3. A box says "controller disconnected". Click it away.
Step 4. You are at the title screen. Decide who hosts:
          one player does PART 3, the other does PART 4.

PART 3: HOST
------------
Step 1. Press H.
Step 2. The box in the top-left now says "Host co-op: ON".
        Under it is your address, like 192.168.1.20.
        Tell your friend that address.
Step 3. Press any key.
Step 4. Choose Human Missions.
Step 5. Press Enter.
Step 6. Press Enter.
Step 7. Press Enter.
Step 8. Movies play. Press Enter until they stop.
Step 9. At "PRESS ANY KEY", press Space.
Step 10. Tell your friend: "I'm in, join now."

PART 4: JOIN
------------
Step 1. Wait until the host says "I'm in, join now."
Step 2. Press J.
Step 3. Type the host's address and :47000, like 203.0.113.5:47000
Step 4. Press Enter.
Step 5. Wait. The game loads the host's mission.
        If it says "Invalid address": press J, type the whole address again,
        press Enter.

PART 5: PLAYING
---------------
  E     use / pick up
  2     hold the weapon you just picked up
  Esc   pause menu (QUIT is the last item)

Something odd? Write down the time.

PROBLEMS
--------
"umu-run is missing"         Do PART 1, Step 2.
"install innoextract"        Do PART 1, Step 2.
"Could not find the game"    Do PART 1, Step 4.
"Wrong game version"         You need the GOG version 1.2.0 files.
Both players must use the same zip (the version is in build/VERSION.txt).
Not on the same home network? You need a VPN like Tailscale to connect.
