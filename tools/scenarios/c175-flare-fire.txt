# Cards 174 and 175 (from c172-flare.txt): the client's flare in hand, then its fire after the throw. Card 172: the client's support flare, alone. The client's server copy earns its tactical support charge from
# objective rewards, not from the tutorial pickup: the host hides BP_TacSuppPickup_BombingRun_Tutorial_C at map load
# (card098 "host hide ... caller exe+0x380231B") in every run, c104e included. So the client does the two steps that
# rewarded it in c104e until its server copy holds "20000 1" (full and ready), then presses Four and fires. Predictions in docs/cards/174-client-flare-in-hand.md and 175-hitclaim-miss-no-round.md. Every state step waits for its log line.
